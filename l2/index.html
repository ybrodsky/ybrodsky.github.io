Interlude craft planner

Recipes come live from l2api.dev (Interlude data). The search returns recipes only — type part of what you want to make, pick the success rate you have, and its whole chain is resolved down to raw materials. Fill in Have anywhere and everything updates — stock of a crafted material also removes the base materials underneath it, and anything shared between several picks is counted once. Items with more than one recipe get a success-rate dropdown on their chip. The recipe scroll itself shows up as a base material, because the data lists it as one. Everything you type, plus a local copy of every recipe fetched, is kept in an IndexedDB database in this browser, so a reload costs no network calls and your counts survive going offline.
